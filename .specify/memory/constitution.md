Only public/internal non-sensitive source and synthetic inputs are permitted.
Do not put credentials, private documents/prompts, patient/participant records,
narratives, staff allegations or access records in artifacts, prompts, logs or Git.

publication, provider spending, migrations, deployment and release. No hook or
assessment/convergence verdict supplies that approval. Independent Review and QA
remain required whenever local rules call for them.
